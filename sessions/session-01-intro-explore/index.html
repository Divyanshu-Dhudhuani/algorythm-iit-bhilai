Bridge · what we do next 45 min

The workflow, in order.

1

Identify — file image.png · exiftool image.png · strings image.png | less

2

PNG chunks — IHDR → tEXt/iTXt → IDAT → IEND. Check after IEND.

3

Embedded files — binwalk image.png · binwalk -e · foremost. Repeat on anything extracted.

4

Pixels — RGB 0–255, LSB, bit-planes 0–7. Live in StegOnline, no install. Then zsteg · steghide if needed.

Keep ready: laptop + browser + sample image.png · no Kali needed11
