📚 Session slide decks

HTML decks open directly — no download needed. New sessions get appended to sessions.json.

🧑‍💻 Student bio pages

Each student owns students/<NAME>-<ID>/index.html + one line in students.json. Click a card to open their page. To get featured, open a PR (see README).

Add your page in 4 steps: 1) Fork repo → 2) copy students/_template/ to students/YourName-YourID/ → 3) edit your index.html → 4) add one line to students.json → open PR. Full steps in README. PRs that follow the format are auto-merged 🤖.
Maintained by ALGORYTHM, IIT Bhilai. Pages deploy via GitHub Actions. Student pages are owned by their authors.
