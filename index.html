Why ALGORYTHM? Seniors and juniors exploring tech together — weekly sessions, small homework, CTFs, showcases. Mission: increase tech culture. No session makes you a master; each one is map + proof + first step + game.

Why bother? Leverage — build once, used by thousands (our attendance app, tapped within days). Optionality — GSoC, internships, research, startups: proof beats marks. Fun — your phone becomes a MIDI looper for ₹0. And the rule for the AI era: use AI — but be good enough to catch it when it's wrong.

So: come with your laptop, leave with a page on this hub. Best bio pages go on stage next session. 🔥

🧑‍💻 Student bio pages

Each student owns students/<NAME>-<ID>-<GithubUsername>/index.html + one line in students.json. To get featured, open a PR (see README).

Add your page in 4 steps: 1) Fork repo → 2) copy students/_template/ to students/YourName-YourID-YourGithubUsername/ → 3) edit your index.html → 4) add one line to students.json → open PR from your own branch. Full steps in README. PRs that follow the format are auto-merged 🤖.
